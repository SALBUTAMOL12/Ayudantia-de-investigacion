## Decisions (2017 imagery)
- **Units**: one point per microdata zone `ID_ZONA_LOC` = (comuna, DC, AREA, ZC_LOC). Urban: zona censal of
  `Microdatos_Manzana.shp`; rural: **localidad** (households are identified only to the localidad), not entidad
  as in the RA's pilot; urban zones grouped within district (the pilot merged same-numbered zones across districts).
  Point = household-weighted mean of manzana/entidad centroids. 15,721 of 16,053 zones located; the 332 others are
  DC 99 / ZC 999 (0.35% of occupied dwellings). Households inside the 2.56 km box: 99.4% urban, 73.8% rural.
- **Collection**: `COPERNICUS/S2_HARMONIZED` (L1C TOA), calendar 2017. L2A SR is too sparse over Chile in 2017
  (4–57 scenes per point vs 32–112 for L1C; SR is complete only from 2019).
- **Composite**: per-pixel median of the 10 bands (as the RA's pilot), full year, cloud probability < 40 (joined on
  system:index), C-correction with SRTM fitted per scene over the batch region at 30 m; uint8 = reflectance·255.
- **Export**: batch mosaics to Drive (`gdrive-personal:eii_s2_2017`), batches = 0.5° grid cells (349 batches,
  median 7 zones, max 1,149); downloaded with rclone and cut to 256×256 per zone.
  Tests (batches 9, 143, 258): 10–16 min each, ~11 EECU-h together, no empty pixels in any zone box.

- First full run: 253/349 batches OK; 93 failed because scenes whose C-correction fit had no valid pixels kept
  integer bands (collection not homogeneous) -> all scenes now cast to float before the fit (no change for the
  batches that had succeeded); resubmitted 2026-11-11. Batches 345–347 (4 zones on San Félix / Juan Fernández)
  have no L1C scene in 2016–2018 and are dropped. Fallback window year ± 1 used only when the year has no scene.
- Result (2026-11-11): 346 batch mosaics (`B_inter/eo/2017/s2_batches/`) and 15,717 zone images (6.0 GB,
  `zones/`). Empty pixels: none, except zone 15453 (Cabo de Hornos, 0 households, 94% empty). Missing: the 4
  island zones (350 households) + 332 unlocated zones. Quality caveat: in Aysén/Magallanes and Chiloé the median
  keeps haze and cloud remnants (persistent cloud; L1C). Possible upgrade: Cloud Score+ masking
  (`GOOGLE/CLOUD_SCORE_PLUS/V1/S2_HARMONIZED`) for the southern batches.

## To do (RA): redo the 2017 zone images before fine-tuning
The current `zones/*.tif` are provisional. Two flaws: (i) boxes are defined and exported in degrees (EPSG:4326, equal
degree pixels), so they are 2.56 km N–S but only 2.56·cos(lat) km E–W (2.02 km at Temuco, 1.54 km at Punta Arenas),
with anisotropic pixels; the `share_hh_in_box` figures above assume a true 2.56 km square and overstate coverage.
(ii) One household-weighted point per rural localidad falls between hamlets when the localidad is dispersed (the
pilot instead had one image per entidad and pooled them per zone).
1. **True squares**: define each box as 2.56 km × 2.56 km in UTM (19S; 18S west of −72°) and export/cut in UTM at 10 m.
2. **Box centres at density peaks** (rural): rasterize Google Open Buildings counts inside the localidad/entidad
   polygon (~20 m grid; prefer the yearly *Open Buildings 2.5D Temporal* layer for the census year), Gaussian blur
   (σ ≈ 300–500 m, check visually), take the argmax. Urban zones: household-weighted manzana centroids are fine.
3. **Set of boxes per zone**: greedy cover: peak → box → remove covered buildings → re-blur → next peak, until
   ~95% of the zone's buildings are covered. Report number of images and household coverage before exporting.
4. **Weights**: each box gets census households (`CANTIDAD_H`) of the units assigned to it (urban: manzanas by nearest
   box centre; rural: localidad households × the box's share of buildings). Weights sum to the zone total.
5. **Model**: as the pilot (BigEarthNet ResNet-50, shared across images), but pool image features with the
   household-weighted mean Σ w_s f_s / Σ w_s (not `.mean(1)`), in fine-tuning and in the 512-d feature export.
   Padding slots get weight 0. Same design for 2024 using the cartography counts.
Re-export with `scripts/eo_ml/gee/s2_export.py` (adapt `box()` and the export `crs`); compute for the first run was
~3–4 h of queue time on project `lbn-chile-496122`.
